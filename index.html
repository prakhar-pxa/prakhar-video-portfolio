About

I'm Prakhar, a video editor who enjoys working across different styles. One day it's a fast, punchy reel for social media, the next it's a slower cinematic piece or a clean product video.

Good editing is mostly invisible. It's the rhythm of the cuts, the way sound supports the picture, and knowing what to leave out. I try to give each project its own pace instead of applying the same look to everything.

If you have footage and an idea, I'd like to hear about it.
